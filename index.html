Institutional evidence governance

Evidence & Assurance Hub

Control the institutional evidence lifecycle: capture, classify, validate, approve, retain, review and dispose. Every item remains connected to its department, service, period, owner and applicable standard.

1 · CaptureRegister the document, dataset, approval, communication or outcome evidence.
2 · ClassifyAssign department, semester, service, confidentiality and evidence type.
3 · ValidateConfirm completeness, accuracy, source, date and accountable owner.
4 · ApproveRecord reviewer, approval status, decision date and any required correction.
5 · RetainApply the PMU-approved retention schedule and preserve access history.
6 · Review / DisposeReview at expiry; archive, extend or securely dispose with authorization.
Retention control: the periods below are configurable management fields—not a substitute for PMU’s approved records-retention schedule, legal requirements, privacy rules or litigation holds.
